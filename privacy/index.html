Privacy Policy

Pocketed

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Pocketed is a budgeting app built to be local-first and private by design. Your financial data lives on your device and leaves it only to a sync server you run yourself, if you choose to set one up. This policy explains what that means for your privacy.

We don't collect your data.

Pocketed has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We never see, store, or transmit your budget.

What we collect

Nothing. All of your budget data — accounts, envelopes, transactions, bills, goals, debt, projects, and forecasts — is stored only on your device in a local database. Using Pocketed does not create an account, and there is no cloud service operated by us behind the app.

A fresh install used in airplane mode has the full feature set and sends zero bytes over the network.

Information you choose to sync (optional, self-hosted)

Pocketed can optionally sync your budget across your own devices. This is entirely optional and off by default. If you turn it on:

  • You run the Pocketed sync server yourself (for example, with one Docker command) on your own home network or a private network such as Tailscale or WireGuard, and point the app at it.
  • Your data is sent only to your own server, over your own network. It never reaches Pocketed, its developers, or any third party.
  • The connection is authenticated with a token that your server generates and that you control, stored in your device's secure storage.
  • Your server stores only your budget data — no email addresses and no account identifiers.
  • You can delete all synced data at any time by deleting your server's data.

If you never set up a server, Pocketed makes no network connections at all.

Tracking and analytics

Pocketed does not track you across apps or websites, and does not include any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or third-party SDKs. We do not use cookies or advertising identifiers. In App Store terms, no data is collected and no data is used to track you.

On-device protection

You can turn on an optional biometric or PIN app lock (Settings → App lock) to require authentication before opening Pocketed. Your PIN is stored only on your device as a salted hash; biometric data never leaves your device and is handled entirely by your operating system. Device-level encryption applies to the app's data according to your operating system settings.

Supporting Pocketed

Pocketed is free. The Settings screen includes an optional "Buy me a coffee" link. Tapping it opens an external website in your browser; any information you provide there is handled by that third party under their own privacy policy. We share no data with them, and the link unlocks nothing — every feature is already free.

Children's privacy

Pocketed is a general-purpose budgeting tool and is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data stays on your device, you control its retention. To delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If you set up self-hosted sync, delete your server's data volume to remove the synced copy. We hold no copy of your data, and there is no account for us to delete.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete your personal data, and not to have it sold. Pocketed honors these by design: your data is in your possession, we never collect or sell it, and we hold nothing to disclose. If you have any question about your data, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised version at this address. Material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.
